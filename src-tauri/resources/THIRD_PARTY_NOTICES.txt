Japanese Sentiment Polarity Dictionary
--------------------------------------

Musical does not bundle these dictionary files. On first use, it retrieves
the two unmodified files below over HTTPS from their official locations,
verifies their pinned SHA-256 digests, and stores them atomically in the
Tauri application cache. Partial, failed, or hash-mismatched downloads are
not treated as usable cache entries.

Author(s): Inui-Okazaki Laboratory, Tohoku University
Official resource page:
https://www.cl.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/Open_Resources-Japanese_Sentiment_Polarity_Dictionary.html

Japanese Sentiment Dictionary (Volume of Verbs and Adjectives) ver. 1.0:
https://www.cl.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/resources/sent_lex/wago.121808.pn
SHA-256: 968b8f758e79531a70b26600e943f11e9fc2962c5c30174b2bc96a342c1bb8b4

Requested citation:
Nozomi Kobayashi, Kentaro Inui, Yuji Matsumoto, Kenji Tateishi.
Collecting Evaluative Expressions for Opinion Extraction, Journal of Natural
Language Processing 12(3), 203-222, 2005.

Japanese Sentiment Dictionary (Volume of Nouns) ver. 1.0:
https://www.cl.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/resources/sent_lex/pn.csv.m3.120408.trim
SHA-256: 94f545a49028c8a07929c0ac35afa56fbfce9e8a277ce70233c6340845439ae3

Requested citation:
Masahiko Higashiyama, Kentaro Inui, Yuji Matsumoto. Learning Sentiment of
Nouns from Selectional Preferences of Verbs and Adjectives, Proceedings of
the 14th Annual Meeting of the Association for Natural Language Processing,
pp. 584-587, 2008.

The official resource page permits commercial use when credit is given.
Musical additionally follows its project policy of not redistributing the raw
dictionary files; they remain runtime downloads in the local application
cache.
